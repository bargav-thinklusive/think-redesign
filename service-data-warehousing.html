The big players, compared

Snowflake, Databricks or AWS? We'll tell you straight.

Every data cloud claims to do everything. In practice, each has a sweet spot. We match the platform to your workloads, skills and budget, not to a partner quota.

Best for SQL-first analytics teams

Snowflake

Near-zero administration, storage and compute that scale separately, and secure data sharing across clouds.

Snowpark · Data sharing & Marketplace · Cortex AI

Best for data science & AI

Databricks

One lakehouse for engineering, streaming, BI and machine learning on open Delta Lake tables.

Delta Lake · Unity Catalog · Mosaic AI

Best for AWS-native estates

Amazon Redshift

Tight integration with S3, Glue and the rest of AWS, with serverless and provisioned options.

Redshift Serverless · Zero-ETL · Redshift ML

Best for zero-ops scale

Google BigQuery

Fully serverless — no clusters to size — with built-in machine learning and pay-per-query pricing.

BigQuery ML · Looker · Gemini in BigQuery
