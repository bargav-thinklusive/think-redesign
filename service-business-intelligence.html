The big players, compared

Power BI, Tableau, Qlik or Looker?

Our BI team has delivered on Cognos, Microsoft BI and SAP BI for years, and on today's cloud leaders too. Each tool shines in a different place — here's how we help clients choose.

Best for Microsoft 365 organizations

Microsoft Power BI

Low cost per user, deep Excel and Teams integration, and one data platform through Microsoft Fabric.

Semantic models & DAX · Fabric · Copilot

Best for visual exploration

Tableau

Drag-and-drop analysis that business users pick up fast, backed by a huge community.

Tableau Cloud · Tableau Pulse · Salesforce integration

Best for associative discovery

Qlik Sense

The associative engine reveals relationships across sources that query-based tools can miss.

Associative engine · Qlik Cloud · Qlik Answers

Best for governed, embedded metrics

Looker

One semantic model, so every team uses the same definitions — and it embeds cleanly in your own products.

LookML · Embedded analytics · Gemini in Looker
